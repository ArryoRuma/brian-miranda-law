# Miranda Law — Brand & Messaging Guide

This file is the source of truth for brand positioning, messaging, voice, audience, and copy decisions for the Miranda Law website.

When writing or editing customer-facing copy, follow this document alongside any applicable copywriting or StoryBrand skills.

---

# 1. Brand

**Name:** Miranda Law

**Industry:** Estate Planning / Legal Services

**Primary Market:** New Jersey

**Primary Practice Focus:** Estate Planning

**Languages / Audiences:** English, Spanish, and Portuguese

Miranda Law helps individuals and families create practical estate plans that protect the people they love and make difficult situations easier for their families.

The brand should combine the **authority and competence expected of an attorney** with the **warmth and humanity appropriate to conversations about family, legacy, protection, and the future.**

---

# 2. Core Brand Idea

## Make things easier for the people you love.

Estate planning is not primarily about documents.

It is about making sure the people someone loves are protected, prepared, and not left trying to figure everything out during an already difficult time.

The emotional center of the Miranda Law brand is:

**Taking care of the people you love by planning ahead.**

---

# 3. Brand Positioning

Miranda Law should not feel like a large, intimidating, corporate law firm.

It should feel like working directly with a knowledgeable attorney who:

- listens
- explains things clearly
- understands the client's concerns
- simplifies an intimidating process
- helps the client make informed decisions
- creates a practical plan
- treats the client's family and circumstances personally

The experience should feel:

**Professional without being stuffy.**

**Personal without being casual.**

**Authoritative without being intimidating.**

**Warm without losing credibility.**

---

# 4. StoryBrand Roles

## The Customer Is the Hero

The client is the protagonist.

They are trying to protect their family, prepare for the future, and make responsible decisions about what happens to the people and assets they care about.

Do not position Miranda Law or the attorney as the hero.

---

## Miranda Law Is the Guide

Miranda Law provides:

### Empathy

Estate planning can feel complicated, uncomfortable, or easy to postpone.

Clients may not know:

- what documents they need
- whether they need a will or trust
- what happens if they do nothing
- who will make decisions if they cannot
- how their assets will be handled
- how to protect their spouse or children
- where to begin

Miranda Law understands these concerns and makes the process easier to navigate.

### Authority

Miranda Law provides the legal knowledge and structured process necessary to turn the client's intentions into a proper estate plan.

Authority should be demonstrated through clarity, competence, process, and expertise rather than boastful language.

---

# 5. Primary Customer

Miranda Law primarily serves individuals, couples, parents, and families in New Jersey who know they should have an estate plan but may not know where to begin.

Common situations include:

- getting married
- having children
- buying a home
- accumulating assets
- starting or owning a business
- caring for aging family members
- experiencing a major life change
- realizing an existing will or estate plan is outdated
- wanting to make things easier for family members
- simply deciding it is time to get their affairs in order

Many clients are not legal experts.

Write accordingly.

---

# 6. Multilingual Audience

A significant portion of Miranda Law's audience speaks Spanish or Portuguese.

English, Spanish, and Portuguese visitors should receive an equally credible and intentional brand experience.

Translated content should prioritize **natural meaning and cultural clarity**, not literal word-for-word translation.

Do not make multilingual audiences feel like an afterthought or secondary market.

Where relevant, the ability to communicate with clients in their preferred language is a meaningful trust advantage.

---

# 7. What the Customer Wants

At the highest level:

**The customer wants confidence that the people they love will be taken care of.**

Supporting desires include:

- knowing their wishes will be respected
- protecting their spouse
- protecting their children
- deciding who receives their assets
- choosing who can make decisions for them
- avoiding unnecessary confusion
- reducing the burden placed on family
- understanding what documents they actually need
- getting the process completed correctly
- having confidence that they have handled something important

---

# 8. The Customer's Problem

## External Problem

They do not have an adequate estate plan.

They may be missing:

- a will
- a trust
- a power of attorney
- a healthcare directive
- beneficiary planning
- coordinated estate documents

Or their existing documents may no longer reflect their life or wishes.

---

## Internal Problem

Estate planning can make people feel:

- uncertain
- overwhelmed
- confused
- uncomfortable
- worried about making the wrong decision
- guilty for putting it off
- unsure where to begin

A major internal tension is:

**"I know I should take care of this, but I don't really know what I need or how to get started."**

---

## Philosophical Problem

The people you love should not have to untangle avoidable legal and financial confusion during an already difficult time.

Planning ahead is an act of responsibility and care.

---

# 9. The Stakes

If someone does not plan ahead, important decisions may be made without clear instructions from them.

Their family may face:

- uncertainty
- delays
- unnecessary legal complexity
- disagreement
- additional stress
- questions about what their loved one actually wanted

Do not exaggerate these consequences.

Do not use fear-based estate planning tactics.

The goal is to communicate the importance of planning without exploiting anxiety about death.

---

# 10. Successful Outcome

After working with Miranda Law, the client should feel:

**Prepared.**

**Relieved.**

**Confident.**

**Responsible.**

**Protected.**

They should be able to think:

> I know my affairs are in order.

> My wishes are documented.

> My family knows what happens next.

> I've made things easier for the people I love.

This transformation is more important than the legal documents themselves.

---

# 11. Services

Primary estate planning services include:

## Wills

Help clients document how they want their assets handled and who should carry out their wishes.

## Trusts

Help clients determine when a trust may be appropriate and structure one around their goals when necessary.

## Powers of Attorney

Allow clients to designate someone they trust to handle financial and legal matters if they cannot act for themselves.

## Health Care Directives

Help clients document healthcare wishes and designate appropriate decision-makers.

When describing services, always connect the legal instrument to **why it matters to the client.**

Do not simply define legal documents.

---

# 12. Messaging Hierarchy

When possible, website messaging should move through this general sequence:

1. What the customer ultimately wants
2. Why estate planning matters
3. The problem with leaving things unresolved
4. Miranda Law as the knowledgeable guide
5. What the client may need
6. A simple process for getting it done
7. The successful outcome
8. A clear call to action

Do not rigidly force this structure onto every page.

---

# 13. Homepage Hero

The hero should quickly communicate:

**What Miranda Law does**

Estate planning.

**Who it is for**

Individuals and families who want to protect the people they love and prepare for the future.

**Why it matters**

Planning now makes things easier for the people you care about later.

**What to do next**

Take a clear step toward starting an estate plan.

The visitor should not need legal knowledge to understand the hero.

---

# 14. Calls to Action

Calls to action should be concrete and low-friction.

The primary CTA should lead toward beginning a conversation or consultation with Miranda Law.

Use consistent CTA terminology throughout the site.

Avoid relying on vague CTAs such as:

- Learn More
- Explore
- Discover
- Get Started

when a more specific action can be used.

Secondary educational CTAs may be appropriate for visitors who are not ready to contact the firm.

---

# 15. Voice

Miranda Law should sound:

- clear
- reassuring
- competent
- warm
- direct
- human
- practical
- trustworthy

The voice should feel like a knowledgeable attorney explaining something important across a table — not a law-school textbook and not an advertising agency.

---

# 16. Writing Style

## Prefer

Plain English.

Short paragraphs.

Clear headings.

Direct explanations.

Specific examples when useful.

Second-person language:

**you**

**your family**

**your wishes**

**your plan**

Explain legal terminology when it must be used.

Connect every service or feature to a customer benefit.

---

## Avoid

Legalese when plain language works.

Corporate language.

Overly formal writing.

Generic marketing language.

Artificial urgency.

Fearmongering.

Overly sentimental writing.

Excessive references to death.

Long explanations before communicating the main point.

Generic AI language such as:

- navigate the complexities
- comprehensive solutions
- tailored solutions
- peace of mind solutions
- seamless process
- unique needs
- safeguard your future
- secure your legacy
- embark on your journey

These phrases are not absolutely prohibited, but they should only be used when they communicate something specific.

---

# 17. Emotional Balance

Estate planning sits between two emotional worlds.

## Law

- authority
- professionalism
- competence
- reliability
- precision

## Family

- safety
- comfort
- warmth
- legacy
- protection
- love

Successful Miranda Law messaging balances both.

Too far toward **law**, and the brand feels like a stuffy corporate law firm.

Too far toward **family**, and the brand loses the authority and credibility expected when hiring an attorney.

Every major copy and design decision should consider this balance.

---

A knowledgeable attorney sitting across the table from a family, explaining an important legal decision in language they actually understand.

Not:

A salesperson.

Not:

A lecturer.

Not:

A corporate law firm.

The visual identity should reinforce the same balance as the messaging.

## Graphite / Dark Neutral

Represents:

- authority
- professionalism
- seriousness
- legal credibility

## Warm White / Sand

Represents:

- warmth
- approachability
- family
- comfort

## Dark Goldenrod / Gold

Primary brand accent.

Represents:

- value
- permanence
- legacy
- protection
- the people and assets an estate plan is designed to protect

### Core Palette

**Dark Goldenrod:** `#A98D3A`

**Graphite:** `#2C2C2C`

Use warm whites and sand tones as supporting neutrals.

Gold should function primarily as an intentional brand accent rather than overwhelming the interface.

---

# 20. Copy Decision Filter

Before publishing customer-facing copy, ask:

### Clarity
Would someone with no legal background understand this?

### Relevance
Does the customer immediately understand why this matters to them?

### Customer Focus
Are we talking about the client's life more than ourselves?

### Benefit
Have we explained why the legal service matters rather than merely what it is?

### Authority
Does Miranda Law sound knowledgeable and trustworthy?

### Humanity
Does it still sound like a real person?

### StoryBrand
Is the customer the hero and Miranda Law the guide?

### Action
Is the next step obvious?

### Brand
Does this balance legal authority with warmth and family?

If the answer to any of these is no, improve the copy before publishing.

Miranda Law's website should leave visitors thinking:

**They understand what matters to me.**

**They can explain this without making it complicated.**

**I understand why I need to take care of this.**

**I trust them to help me do it correctly.**

**I know what my next step is.**

Above all:

## Make things easier for the people you love.
